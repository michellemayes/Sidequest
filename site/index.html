Hover any message, open Sidequest, and click a quest or press its number. The agent gets the message, the thread, who sent it, and any screenshots or logs attached.

Loot: an explanation. Changes nothing.

Loot: a branch with commits on it.

Loot: an answer, with receipts.

How it works

It rides on the Slack you already have open.

Slack's desktop app is built on Electron. Sidequest slips a small overlay into it over the DevTools Protocol, and a daemon on your Mac does the rest. No Slack app. Nobody to ask.

🌱
STEP 1

A fresh worktree

git worktree add on a new branch, so your own checkout stays exactly as you left it.

📜
STEP 2

A full prompt

The message, its thread, the permalink and any attached files, written into the worktree.

🚀
STEP 3

Your agent starts

In a new terminal tab, in the background, or in the Claude or ChatGPT desktop app.

🔔
STEP 4

Slack keeps up

Commits, the pull request and the agent's reply show up right on the message.

Assemble your party

Bring the agent you already like.

Base camp

A Mac app for keeping an eye on the party.

Start quests from Slack, follow them in the app. Sessions are sorted by what needs you, and the Dock icon counts what's waiting.

  • Edit and post the agent's reply, or send a follow-up, without leaving the session.
  • Headless sessions get a live conversation, the diff, and a terminal.
  • Signed, notarized and self-updating. It carries its own engine, so it's the only thing to install.
